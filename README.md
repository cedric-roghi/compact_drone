## Rationale
The design idea for this drone is not my own. Despite it being a rare design I got inspiration from projects such as [backcountrydrones](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=82phDhYGT_0) and [Tdrone](https://github.com/ShenZhenAccelerationTechCo/Tdrone). The NASA mars copter is another example of a contra rotating drone design.

The idea is that the propellers can fold down which means the drone can fit in smaller storage solutions. Another idea would be to tube launch the drone for rapid remote deployment from a ground or air vehicle.

- STM32f411:
    - Same MCU I used for my turret project. Its fast, powerful, and has many timers for pwm output. Since I used it for the turret it makes the PCB development for the drone much faster.
